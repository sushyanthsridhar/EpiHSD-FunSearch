Stage 3 — Mutation Operators

  • perturb(eq) — add Gaussian noise (σ=0.1) to one randomly chosen coefficient
  • add_term(eq) — append a random feature from the library with a small random coefficient (drawn from N(0, 0.2))
  • remove_term(eq) — drop the term with the smallest absolute coefficient
  • swap_feature(eq) — replace one feature with a random different feature, keeping the coefficient
  • crossover(eq_a, eq_b) — randomly merge terms from two parent equations (each term included with p=0.5)
  • Mutation is selected randomly with configurable probabilities; equations with > 10 terms are forced to remove_term

Evolution Loop
Tournament selection · N=200 generations · migration every 25 gen

Stage 5 — Evolution Loop

  • Each generation: sample 2 parents by tournament selection (k=3) → apply mutation → evaluate → replace weakest if improvement
  • Selection pressure: tournament size 3 — balanced between exploration and exploitation
  • Migration every 25 generations: copy top-1 equation from Island A into Island B population and vice versa
  • Early stopping: if best fitness has not improved by 0.005 R² in 50 consecutive generations, halt
  • Logging: save fitness history to evolution_log.csv every 10 generations; save best equation per island per generation
  • Target: beat SINDy baseline mean test R² of 0.39 on 2022 validation set

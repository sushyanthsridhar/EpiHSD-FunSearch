LLM-SINDy-FunSearch

DR Dengue — LLM-powered equation discovery in latent space.

End goal. Understand the true dynamics of dengue in the Dominican Republic. Discover governing equations that are sparse, interpretable, and possibly reveal something biology did not already know. The model should earn its results, not just fit a curve.

Stop 1
Clean Weekly Data
input.csv. 14,976 rows. 32 DR provinces. 2015–2023. Cases, temperature, rainfall, humidity, population.
Stop 2
SINDy Baseline
Per-province and pooled equations found. R² floors established. Temperature appears only as interaction terms, not standalone. Coastal vs inland regime split discovered independently.
↓
Stop 3
GA-SINDy + LLM Mutator
Genetic algorithm over islands with simulated annealing. BO-tuned hyperparameters. Plateaus at epoch 6 due to fixed 66-term library ceiling. Structural mutator replaced with LLM in next phase.
Stop 4
FunSearch with Island Clustering
Geographic island split. Coastal and inland equations discovered. Spectral fit scores 0.83 to 0.93. Delta over SINDy baseline confirmed positive for most provinces.
↓
Phase 1 — Reshape input into tensor
Stop 5
Province-Year Chunking
Reshape input.csv from a flat 14,976-row table into 288 chunks. 32 provinces times 9 years. Each chunk is 52 weeks times 15 features, including dI/dt. Each chunk is one independent outbreak episode.
Stop 5b
Chunking Strategy
Yearly chunks are the default. But epidemic cycles do not respect calendar years. Could chunk by outbreak phase instead. Or use adaptive windowing based on case thresholds. How you cut here shapes everything downstream.
↓
Phase 2 — Autoencoder compression
Stop 6
Encoder
Each 52x15 chunk fed through layered compression. 780 numbers funneled down through hidden layers to 3 latent coordinates. Trained by reconstruction error. Learns its own coordinate system for outbreak shape.
Stop 7
Decoder
Mirror of the encoder. Takes 3 latent numbers and reconstructs the full 52x15 chunk. The decoder is what translates latent equations back into dI/dt in the original feature space.
↓
Checkpoint A
3D Latent Space Visualization
288 province-year chunks become 288 points in a 3D scatter plot. Rotatable, colorable by province, year, regime, burden level. The invisible structure of DR dengue dynamics becomes visually accessible for the first time. This visualization alone is a contribution.
↓
Stop 7b
Encoder Architecture Search
Number of layers, layer sizes, bottleneck dimension, activation functions are all choices. The LLM could propose encoder architectures the same way it proposes equation terms. Nobody has done architecture search for scientific autoencoders via LLM. This is an open innovation point.
↓
Phase 3 — SINDy in latent space
Stop 8
Latent Library Construction
Library terms built automatically from z1, z2, z3. All polynomials up to degree 2. Roughly 15 to 20 terms. No domain knowledge needed. No manual curation. A fraction of the original 66-term library.
Stop 9
SINDy on Latent Coordinates
SINDy finds dz/dt as a sparse function of z1, z2, z3. The governing equation of outbreak shape evolution. Output is a clean interpretable equation in latent space. Decoder then maps it back to dI/dt.
↓
Checkpoint B
SINDy Autoencoder for Epidemiology
The combination of autoencoder compression and SINDy equation discovery exists in physics and fluid dynamics. Champion et al. 2019. It has never been applied to vector-borne disease dynamics. This is the core methodological novelty of the paper.
↓
Phase 4 — GA islands and LLM mutation in latent space
Stop 10
GA Islands in Latent Space
3 to 5 islands each running SINDy independently on the same 288-point latent dataset. Islands evolve different sparse equations in parallel. GA coordinator picks top 2 offspring and combines them each epoch.
Stop 11
LLM Mutator with Rich Context
LLM receives current best equation, per-province R² scores, which mutations succeeded in past epochs, and biological context about what the latent coordinates represent. Proposes structurally meaningful mutations, not blind add or remove.
↓
Checkpoint C
Dynamic Library Expansion
Instead of selecting from a fixed library, let the LLM propose new functional forms, sigmoid of z1, exponential of z2, threshold functions. Validated new terms get added to the library for future generations. Turns SINDy from fixed-basis search into evolving-basis search. No published work does this.
↓
Stop 11b
Mutation Memory
Track which LLM mutations improved fitness across epochs. Feed that history back into the next prompt. The LLM learns within a run which directions the landscape rewards. In-context meta-learning on equation search. Open question: how far back should the memory reach?
Stop 11c
Cross-Disease Transfer Prior
Zika and chikungunya share the Aedes aegypti vector with dengue. Their latent dynamics may be structurally similar. Prime the LLM with published equations from those diseases as a prior. Transfer learning at the equation level. Has never been attempted.
↓
Phase 5 — Discovery and interpretation
Stop 12
Decode Back to dI/dt
Once the best latent equation is found, pass it through the decoder to recover the governing equation in original feature space. This is the biological result. The equation for how dengue incidence changes week to week, discovered from shape dynamics.
Stop 13
Biological Interpretation Pass
Ask the LLM to read the decoded equation and explain what it is saying biologically. Which features dominate. What it implies about seasonality. Whether it independently recovered known mechanisms like EIP temperature dependence or found something new.
↓
Checkpoint D
Hypothesis-Validation Loop
After each generation the LLM reads the best equation and writes a falsifiable prediction. Test it against held-out data. Equations that generate validated predictions earn a fitness bonus. Science inside the loop, not just curve fitting. This closes the discovery cycle.
↓
Phase 6 — Forward projection and optimization
Stop 14
Forward Tensor Projection
Use the validated governing equations to simulate forward 4 to 8 weeks. Generate a future tensor of province by week by features. This is the forecast. Not a statistical extrapolation but a physics-based simulation driven by discovered equations.
Stop 15
Resource Allocation Optimization
Which provinces will surge simultaneously? Where is the gap between projected cases and healthcare capacity? Use the future tensor to drive pre-positioning of medical supplies, vector control deployment, and public health alerts.
↓
Stop 15b
Climate Scenario Simulation
Run the same equations under plus 1 and plus 2 degree Celsius warming scenarios. What happens to outbreak timing, magnitude, and geographic spread? This turns a modelling paper into a public health contribution with direct policy relevance for the Dominican Republic.
Checkpoint E
Final Discovery Audit
Before writing up, pause. Did the model recover known biology without being told? Did it find something new? Did LLM-proposed terms outperform the polynomial baseline? Did the latent space cluster provinces in a way that makes epidemiological sense? If yes on any of these, that is the paper.
What success looks like. A sparse interpretable equation for dengue dynamics in the Dominican Republic, discovered through latent space evolution, that makes biological sense, validates on held-out data, and says something about the system that was not explicitly programmed in. The equations drive a forward simulation that is useful for real public health decisions.

dI/dt CORRELATION ANALYSIS
============================================================

WHAT IS dI/dt
----------------------------------------
dI/dt is the weekly rate of change of dengue incidence.
It is the quantity SINDy will learn to model.
Understanding what drives it tells us which terms
should appear in the governing equation library.

LAG ANALYSIS SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
Lag 0: contemporaneous effect (same week)
Lag 1: does last week predict this week dI/dt?
If lag 0 is stronger than lag 1, the relationship is
concurrent, not predictive. If lag 1 is similar to lag 0,
there is genuine predictive signal worth including in SINDy.
